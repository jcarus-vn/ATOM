02 /  What ATOM builds

One concept per note, each with its source. Cross-checked concepts merge into one consensus note. Disagreements stay visible as contested, with both sides and their sources, instead of being silently picked for you.

The index routes a question straight to the right note. Measured on a 118-concept master skill: 71 tokens through the taxonomy against 2,201 scanning flat. That gap is the product.

Concepts are linked by typed edges, so the agent can follow a relationship instead of guessing one. The measured master skill carries 124 edges across its 118 concepts.

Facets slice the same brain by angle, and the registry keeps every master skill findable. One field, one master skill. Ask in one field and only that field loads.

Photographed and scanned PDFs go through real OCR instead of producing empty skills. Vietnamese and English measured at 0.3% character error, with a cache that makes a re-run far faster.

Ask inside exactly one document. A bound view answers from that text alone and never blends in the rest of the field. Built for regulations, where the exact document is the answer.

03 /  In the beginning

Every turn, the agent re-reads the PDF, re-finds the chapter, re-pays the token bill, and still guesses when the answer is not in front of it. Your agent does not have a reading problem. It has a memory problem.

04 /  How it works

Three moves. One pass.

01

Extract

Point it at a file, folder, or glob. The deterministic extractor pulls clean text and metadata, picks the right tool for the book, and OCRs the scans that have no text layer at all.

Formats PDF, EPUB, DOCX, MD, HTML, RTF, MOBI

02

Distill

The generator turns the text into atomic notes and builds the skill: frameworks, decision rules, anti-patterns, per-chapter files. Structure, not a summary. Conflicts stay contested.

Built notes, taxonomy, typed graph, facets, registry

03

Query

Your agent loads the skill on demand and answers from the real content. One field, one master skill. A bound view answers from one exact document and nothing else.

Ask /my-book replication, routed in 71 tokens
